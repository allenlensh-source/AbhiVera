Why Thousands Choose ABHIVERA Over Buying

From short-term party cooking to testing gadgets or earning passive income, here is why renting and "selling as rent" makes total sense.

01

Save Up To 85% Cash

Why spend ₹25,000–₹50,000 on home appliances you only use once or twice a year? Borrow for as low as ₹90–₹350/day and keep your hard-earned money in your bank.

02

"Sell as Rent" (Rent-to-Own)

Owners can list items to sell via rent! Renters can "try before buying" or buyout the item outright at a pre-set price if they fall in love with it during their rental.

03

100% Deposit Protection

All security deposits are safely held in escrow. As soon as the item is returned in working condition, your deposit is refunded automatically without deductions.

04

Mandatory KYC Verification

Zero anonymous users. Every lender and borrower must sign in and verify their government identity (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport) to ensure neighborhood trust.

05

Sanitized & Condition Graded

Every appliance, utensil set, and guest linen bundle is inspected, deep-cleaned, and graded (*Brand New, Like New, Good*) with accessory checklists.

06

Passive Income for Lenders

Turn unused blenders, projectors, spare mattresses, and party pots into an asset earning ₹1,500 to ₹8,000 every single month from verified neighbors.
